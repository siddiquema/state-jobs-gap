  • This is the official national household survey, published by the National Statistics Office. It is an average, not the median you asked for. A median needs the survey's unit-level files.
  • West Bengal's urban average is Rs 19,019 against Rs 26,247 for India. Kolkata's city average is Rs 19,093.
  • Telangana, Karnataka, Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu range from Rs 23,798 to Rs 27,169, up to 1.63 times West Bengal's.
  • The figures come from a sample survey, so small differences between states are uncertain; the gap to India is large.

Question 5 · Sector-wise pay

Raising median pay by sector starts with measuring why pay is low; national pay differs four-fold by occupation, so the mix of jobs matters

Exhibit 2: India: average monthly wage or salary of regular employees, by occupation

Rupees a month, rural and urban, all persons, 2025

A measure-then-target method

1. Measure the baselineMedian monthly regular wage by sector and occupation for West Bengal and for the comparison states, from the survey's unit-level files, with the margin of error.
2. Find out why pay is lowSplit the gap to a comparison state into two parts: the state has people in lower-paying kinds of work (mix), or pays less for the same work (within-sector). The remedies differ.
3. Choose sectors and set targetsPick the sectors where the state has many jobs or many new jobs and the pay gap is largest. Set yearly targets for each sector's median, with a published baseline.
4. Publish every yearReport the median by sector against the target and against other states, so that progress can be checked by anyone.
Team proposal No promise is made about what any policy would achieve. The data to run steps 1 and 2 for West Bengal is not in the public reports reviewed.
Source: PLFS 2025 annual report, Table 51 (printed page A-295; all India, rural and urban, persons); team analysis

Limits of this note

What this note does not do

These figures are not ratified by the state government.They are official national statistics. Ratification by the state is a separate step, and the source table lets the state's statistics department do it.
Averages are not medians.Question 3 asked for the median. The reports give averages; the median needs the survey's unit-level files.
Nothing about 2027 and 2028 is estimated.No forecast, pipeline or company list is given, because none was found in public data.
Statements about named companies and ministers were not checked.Investments by named firms, real-estate and power projects, the absence of global capability centres, and the comparison with other states' leadership were raised in your message. None of it is used here, and this note takes no position on policy or on individuals.
The earlier report stands, with its limits.It measures formal job entry per young person and job quality. It does not measure where graduates go, which is why the tracer study on its page 16 matters, and it does not cover short- and long-term job opportunities, which is the gap the requests on page 5 are meant to fill.
