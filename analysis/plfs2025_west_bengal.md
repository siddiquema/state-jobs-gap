# PLFS 2025: what it says about West Bengal graduates

Sources: PLFS Annual Report 2025 (Tables 22-24); Labour Market Snapshot of Selected Districts; Labour Market Dynamics in Million-plus Cities.
Usual Status (ps+ss), persons aged 15+, rural+urban. Data: `data/plfs2025_education_by_state.csv`, `data/plfs2025_district_snapshot.csv`, `data/plfs2025_kolkata_howrah_vs_benchmarks.csv`.

## By education level, West Bengal vs all India (%)
| Education | LFPR WB | LFPR India | WPR WB | WPR India | UR WB | UR India |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Graduate | 65.9 | 66.2 | 58.4 | 58.8 | **11.4** | 11.2 |
| Post graduate & above | 72.0 | 70.7 | 67.5 | 63.6 | **6.3** | 10.0 |
| Diploma/certificate | 82.1 | 78.6 | 72.6 | 73.2 | **11.6** | 6.9 |
| All levels | 59.1 | 59.3 | 57.4 | 57.4 | **2.8** | 3.1 |

## Reading it
- Overall unemployment is low (2.8%), but for **graduates it is 11.4%**, about four times the all-level rate, and in line with India's 11.2%. Low headline unemployment hides graduate unemployment.
- Post-graduates fare better in West Bengal (6.3%) than nationally (10.0%).
- Diploma/certificate holders do worse than nationally (11.6% vs 6.9%).
- **Kolkata (city corporation)** has a low unemployment rate (1.9%, high RSE) but low pay: average regular wage/salary Rs 19,093 a month vs Rs 28,808 for all million-plus cities and Rs 26,258 for urban India. Female regular-wage earnings are Rs 13,784. Low unemployment with low earnings suggests people take whatever work is available rather than that good jobs are plentiful. This is an inference, not a finding of the survey.
- Youth NEET (15-29): West Bengal 29.9%; Kolkata 21.9% (female 44.2%); most other districts 31-38%.

## Cautions
- Graduate LFPR and WPR in West Bengal match India's; the survey does not show graduates leaving the state. PLFS measures where people are surveyed, not where they studied.
- UR figures for small groups (e.g. West Bengal urban female graduates) have high relative standard errors; see Appendix B of the annual report before quoting sub-groups.
- Kolkata/Howrah city figures rest on small samples (the report flags high-RSE estimates with *).
